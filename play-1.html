A TAYLOR FOR LADIES

SYNOPSIS

Dr Moulineaux finds himself in a pickle: after a night out meeting his mistress, he returns home to discover his wife has found his bed empty. To cover his tracks he takes on a disguise as a ladies’ tailor.

In doing so, he plunges himself into a web of lies, mistaken identities, far-fetched disguises and social frivolity and it may be that he has gone just a bit too far this time.

Broadmai Productions +1 The tone is that of a French farce: extravagant, camp, set in high-class society, full of self-indulgence, deceit, and comedic mayhem.
